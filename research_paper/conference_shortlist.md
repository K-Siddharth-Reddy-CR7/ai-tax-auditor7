## Verification checklist per candidate
Each item below is checked separately, because IEEE sponsorship ≠ IEEE Xplore publication ≠ Scopus indexing:
1. Official site, plus the IEEE conference record number in the IEEE Conference Search.
2. Scope fit for an applied AI / decision-support / data-engineering evaluation paper.
3. Deadline with timezone; conference dates and format.
4. **Page limit** and whether references count, with ≤ 6 pages required. Note whether extra pages are allowed or charged.
5. Registration and publication fees.
6. IEEE Xplore history: previous editions present in Xplore.
7. Scopus: the previous year's proceedings appear in Scopus Sources or Scopus search. Advertised "Scopus indexed" claims are not accepted as evidence.
8. Red flags: guaranteed acceptance, very short review time, fee-first submission, unrelated scope.
